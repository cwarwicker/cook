# Overnight Oats
(Note: This recipe is for 2 servings)

## Nutritional Information (Base)
|  |  |
|--|--|
| Protein | 19 |
| Carbs | 32 |
| Fat | 7 |
| **Calories** | **278** |

(approx - per serving)

## Ingredients
**Base** - Rolled oats, Milk, Chia Seeds, Vanilla Protein Powder

You can add whatever extra toppings you want in the morning. I've listed a few of my favourites at the bottom.

## Instructions (for 5 servings)

### Preparation
1. Get a large kilner jar, or similiar
2. Pour in 200g of rolled oats, 50g of vanilla protein powder, 25g of chia seeds, and 750ml of milk
3. Stir it all up until it's all mixed
4. Seal jar and place in the fridge

### Serving

In the morning, simply pour out 1/5th of the mixture into a bowl and add your desired toppings.

#### Apple Cinnamon Raisin
|  |  |
|--|--|
| Protein | 21 |
| Carbs | 90 |
| Fat | 8 |
| **Calories** | **535** |

**Ingredients**

Medium Apple (1), Raisins (30g), Cinnamon (1 tsp), Honey (10g)

**Instructions**

Prior to getting the base mixture out of the jar:

1. Chop the apple up into small pieces and place into a bowl. Pour in the honey and cinnamon. Mix together.
2. Microwave for about 60-90 seconds until the apple is nice and soft
3. Then pour in your base oat mixture
4. Finally, add the raisins on top.
